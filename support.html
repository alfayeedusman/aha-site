Questions, problems or ideas: contact us here (opens a support request). We usually reply within a few days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. aha! has no account and no cloud.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

In aha!, open Settings → Backup & restore → Create backup, choose a password and save the .aha file to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, choose Restore from a backup and enter the same password.

I forgot my backup password.

The file can't be opened without it, by you or by us. Create a new backup with a password you'll keep.

Face ID won't open the Secret Vault.

Tap "Use iPhone passcode". Your passcode always works.

Voice doesn't work.

Your iPhone may not support on-device recognition for your language. Type instead, or turn on Online voice fallback in Settings (audio then goes to Apple for transcription).

How do I delete my data?

Delete a memory from its page, use Settings → Delete all memories, or delete the app.
