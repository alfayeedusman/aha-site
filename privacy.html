Privacy policy

aha! — Where I Put It · Effective November 2026

The short version

aha! does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you remember stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export or back it up.

What aha! stores on your iPhone

The memories you save: names, places, boxes, notes, "also called" names, location history, finds, photos you take or choose, and your settings. They are stored only inside the app on this iPhone and protected by iOS Data Protection.

Camera and photos

aha! uses the camera only when you choose Snap it. Photos you take or pick from your library are saved only inside aha! and are never uploaded.

Microphone and speech

aha! listens only while you use Say it or voice search, and turns speech into text on your iPhone when iOS supports it. If you turn on Online voice fallback in Settings (off by default), your iPhone may send audio to Apple for transcription, under Apple's privacy policy. aha! never saves or sends recordings itself.

Face ID and passcode

Face ID and your passcode are checked by iOS. aha! only learns whether the check succeeded. It never receives your face data or passcode.

Secret Vault

Private memories stay on this iPhone like all other memories. They open only after Face ID or your passcode, are hidden from Home, search, Timeline and place previews, and are never included in plain exports.

Backups and exports

A backup is a file you create and keep wherever you choose, encrypted with a password only you know. aha! never stores or sends that password, and nobody, including us, can open the file without it. An export is an unencrypted spreadsheet with photos that you share yourself; it never includes Secret Vault memories.

iPhone backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or a computer, iOS may include aha!'s data in that backup. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you.

Children

aha! does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a single memory, delete all memories or private memories in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on this iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the web page linked from the App Store, with a new effective date.
