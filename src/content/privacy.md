## 1. Who we are

aha! is made by %%companyLegalName%%, %%address%% ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: [%%privacyEmail%%](mailto:%%privacyEmail%%).

## 2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. aha! has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't send your memories, photos, searches or usage to us or to anyone else.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Everything you save stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection:

- Item names, places, boxes, notes and nicknames you enter
- Photos you take in the app and the pins you place on them
- Location history and found/moved events (these are spots in your home, not GPS locations; aha! never uses your device location)
- Your settings, such as theme, app lock and Secret Vault preferences

If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in your device backup under Apple's own privacy terms. We have no access to it.

## 4. Permissions

aha! asks only when you first use a feature, and you can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings.

- **Camera:** to photograph where you put things. Photos are saved only inside aha!.
- **Photo library** (optional): to choose an existing photo. aha! copies only the photo you pick.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** to turn what you say into text. Audio is not saved.
- **Face ID:** to lock the app or Secret Vault. Face data is handled entirely by iOS; aha! only receives a yes/no result.

## 5. Voice recognition

When your iPhone supports it, speech is converted to text on the device. If on-device recognition isn't available, aha! uses Apple's online speech recognition **only if you turn on "Online voice fallback"** in Settings (off by default). In that case your audio is sent to Apple and handled under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). It is never sent to us.

## 6. Backups and exports

Backups are created only when you ask. They're encrypted with a password you choose; we never see, store or receive that password and can't recover it. You decide where a backup goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop) using the iOS share sheet.

"Export my data" creates an unencrypted spreadsheet and photos so you can read your data anywhere. Secret Vault memories are never included in exports.

## 7. Secret Vault

Private memories are hidden from Home, regular search, the timeline, place previews, widgets, Siri suggestions, notifications and exports, and open only after Face ID or your device passcode. They're included in encrypted backups only.

## 8. Purchases

aha! is a one-time paid download. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; we don't receive your payment details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## 9. Third parties

aha! contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. The only outside services involved are Apple's, as described above (App Store, optional online speech recognition, iCloud Backup if you use it).

## 10. Your choices and deleting data

You can edit or delete any memory, delete all private memories, or delete everything from Settings. Deleting the app removes all its data from your iPhone. Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side, but you're welcome to contact us with any request.

## 11. Children

aha! is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 12. Security

Data is stored in the app's sandbox with iOS file protection. Backups use strong, standard encryption. No method is perfectly secure, so please keep your iPhone protected with a passcode and keep your backup password safe.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change how aha! handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above, and describe meaningful changes in the app's release notes.
